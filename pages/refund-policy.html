Refund Policy

What's refundable, what isn't, and how to request a refund.

Home › Refund Policy

Effective date: 10 June 2026

1. Overview

We aim to be transparent about what is refundable and what is not, so you can engage us with confidence. This Policy applies to service fees paid to My Global Mentors.

2. Free Services

Initial counselling and profile evaluation are free of charge — no payment, no refund involved.

3. Always Non-Refundable

(a) Fees paid to third parties — university application fees, embassy/visa fees, test-booking fees, courier and document charges — whether paid directly by you or through us; (b) services already rendered — for example, applications already prepared and submitted, or visa files already prepared and filed; (c) cases terminated for submission of false information or forged documents.

4. When You Are Eligible for a Refund

Duplicate or erroneous payment — full refund of the duplicate amount.

Cooling-off period — if you cancel within 7 days of payment and before we have started work on your case, you receive a full refund.

Service not delivered — if My Global Mentors fails to deliver the agreed service (for example, no application submitted within the agreed scope), you receive a proportionate refund of the undelivered portion.

5. Visa Refusal

Visa decisions rest solely with the embassy or consulate. Since our service — preparation, documentation, and filing — is fully rendered by the time a decision is made, service fees are not refundable upon visa refusal. However, if your visa is refused, we will support one re-application at no additional service fee (third-party costs such as the new visa fee remain payable by you).

6. How to Request a Refund

Email info@myglobalmentors.com from your registered email with your name, payment reference, and reason. We will acknowledge your request, decide it within 7 business days, and process approved refunds to the original payment method within 7–14 business days.
