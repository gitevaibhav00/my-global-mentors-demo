Our Story

15+ years of trusted mentorship

My Global Mentors began with a simple belief — every Indian student deserves transparent, honest guidance when pursuing their dream of studying abroad. We've spent over a decade building deep university connects, training certified counsellors, and turning study-abroad aspirations into life-defining journeys.

From a small office in India to a network spanning 30+ countries, we've stayed true to one promise: your dream is our mission.
