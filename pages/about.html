Our Story

A decade of trusted mentorship

My Global Mentors was founded on a simple belief — every student deserves honest, transparent guidance when pursuing a world-class education abroad. Built by the team behind BookMyColleges.com, we blend technology-driven counselling with deeply personal mentorship to turn study-abroad aspirations into life-defining journeys.

From university shortlisting and scholarships to applications, education loans, visa processing and test preparation, we support students end-to-end across 30+ destinations — guided by one promise: your dream is our mission.

Meet our Founders

Experienced education leaders driving My Global Mentors with vision, integrity and a student-first philosophy.

Mr. Gaurav Jain

Founder & Managing Director, My Global Mentors

Gaurav Jain is a visionary entrepreneur, education consultant, and industry leader dedicated to transforming the higher-education ecosystem in India and abroad. As the Founder & CEO of BookMyColleges.com — one of India's fastest-growing counselling and admissions platforms — he helps students make informed academic and career decisions through technology-driven, AI-based guidance.

Since its inception in 2016, BookMyColleges.com has counselled over 2,00,000 students, processed more than 50,000 applications, and facilitated over 13,500 successful admissions. Under his leadership, the platform expanded into full study-abroad services — university shortlisting, scholarships, applications, education loans, visa processing and test preparation.

His work has earned wide recognition, including the Best Startup in Higher Education Consultancy Award (Indian Business Awards, 2018) and the globally recognised THE BIZZ Business Excellence Award (2019). He has also been named among the “35 Under 35” leaders and invited as a TEDx speaker at TEDxGateway UnPlugged.

Today, as a founder of My Global Mentors, Gaurav continues to champion innovation, ethical counselling, and a smarter, more accessible global-education ecosystem for learners everywhere.

Ms. Dipali Gaikwad

Co-Founder & Executive Director, My Global Mentors

Dipali Gaikwad is a passionate education leader and international-admissions expert with over a decade of experience in teaching, student counselling, and overseas-education consulting. Driven by the belief that the right guidance can transform lives, she has dedicated her career to helping students make academic decisions that lead to meaningful, successful careers.

A graduate of Fergusson College, Dipali began her journey as an educator before expanding into international education consulting — recognising the need for ethical, transparent and personalised guidance for students pursuing global opportunities. She has worked with leading organisations including Edwise International, AECC and SI-UK.

Her expertise spans university admissions, visa guidance, scholarships and international education systems across the UK, Ireland, Australia, the USA, Canada, Germany and other top destinations. What sets her apart is her ability to blend compassionate counselling with strategic thinking — helping each student choose pathways aligned to their strengths, ambitions and long-term career goals.

Today, as a founder of My Global Mentors, Dipali leads with purpose — combining educational expertise, business acumen and a student-first philosophy to ensure every student gains access to the right guidance and opportunities for a successful future.

Mission · Vision · Values

What drives My Global Mentors

Our Mission

My Global Mentors is committed to empowering students with personalized, ethical, and future-focused education guidance that helps them make informed academic and career decisions. We strive to bridge the gap between aspirations and opportunities by providing transparent counseling, global admissions support, career mentoring, and technology-driven solutions. Our mission is to simplify the educational journey for students and families through expert guidance, innovative tools, and unwavering support. By fostering confidence, clarity, and access to quality education worldwide, we aim to help every learner unlock their full potential and build a successful, meaningful, and globally competitive future.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted student success and education mentoring ecosystem, transforming the way learners discover, access, and achieve educational opportunities. We envision a future where every student, regardless of background or geography, has access to the right guidance, resources, and global pathways needed to achieve their ambitions. Through innovation, technology, and human-centered mentorship, My Global Mentors aims to create a transparent, inclusive, and connected education landscape that nurtures lifelong learning, empowers future leaders, and contributes to building a more skilled, knowledgeable, and globally engaged generation.

Our Values

At My Global Mentors, our values define who we are and guide every decision we make. We uphold integrity through honest, transparent, and ethical counseling practices. We place students at the center of everything, ensuring personalized guidance tailored to their unique goals and strengths. We embrace innovation by leveraging technology and industry insights to improve educational outcomes. We are committed to excellence in service, continuous learning, and professional growth. We believe in inclusivity, empowering students from diverse backgrounds with equal opportunities. Above all, we foster trust, accountability, compassion, and collaboration to create lasting positive impact in students' lives and careers.
