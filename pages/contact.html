FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes — your first profile evaluation and counselling session is completely free, with no obligation to sign up afterwards.

How long does the entire study-abroad process take?

Typically 6 – 12 months from start to departure, depending on your destination, intake season, and how prepared your documents are.

Do you help with education loans?

Absolutely. We have tie-ups with leading public & private banks and NBFCs for both collateral and non-collateral education loans at competitive rates.

What if my visa gets rejected?

While our visa success rate is 98%, in the rare event of rejection we help analyze the reason, prepare a stronger reapplication, or suggest alternate destinations. You're never alone in the process.

Do you offer post-landing support?

Yes — accommodation help, SIM cards, bank account setup, airport pickup coordination, and ongoing mentorship even after you've landed.
